# Source Inventory

## Commerce

- Platform: Shopify Plus
- Purpose:
  - Orders
  - Order lines
  - Refunds
  - Customer purchase history
  - Products
- Initial priority: Phase 1

## Subscriptions

- Platform: Appstle
- Purpose:
  - Subscription contracts
  - Renewals
  - Pauses
  - Skips
  - Cancellations
  - Reactivations
- Initial priority: Phase 2

## Paid Advertising

### Meta Ads

- Purpose:
  - Advertising spend
  - Campaign / ad set / ad reporting
  - Platform-attributed results
- Initial priority: Phase 1
- First automated advertising connector

## Affiliate / Partner Marketing

### Everflow

- Purpose:
  - Affiliate / partner activity
  - Commission or cost reporting
  - Platform-attributed conversions
- Initial priority: Phase 2

## Manual Marketing Spend

- Current system: None
- Proposed initial system: controlled CSV or spreadsheet
- Database remains the reporting source after validation/import
- Initial priority: Phase 1

Examples may include:
- Sponsorships
- Creator placements
- Fixed-fee partnerships
- Agency fees
- Offline campaigns
- Other marketing costs not captured by automated connectors

## Website Analytics

- Platform: GA4
- GA4 currently in use: Yes
- BigQuery export: No
- Initial priority: Phase 2
